You are given the per-question grades from the interview. Base your summary only on those; you have not seen the answers yourself.

Write 2 to 4 sentences covering where the candidate was strongest, where they were weakest, and whether the pattern across questions is consistent or uneven. Name technologies specifically. Do not restate the scores — the reader can see them.

Then give one recommendation, exactly one of:

- `strong_proceed` — clearly meets the bar, advance without reservation
- `proceed` — meets the bar, advance
- `borderline` — evidence is mixed or coverage too thin to decide; a second conversation would settle it
- `do_not_proceed` — does not meet the bar for the stated seniority
